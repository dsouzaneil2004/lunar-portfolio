Chapter 02

Who I Am

I've always been drawn to things
that reward obsession.

I started out wanting to make games. My laptop had other plans. That constraint pushed me toward the web — and somewhere in that pivot I discovered something I hadn't expected: a browser can be a canvas, not just a document. I build with every tool available to me, including AI, not as a shortcut but as a collaborator. An idea deserves to exist even if your hardware is modest.

I grew up in Madikeri, Coorg. On weekends I'm usually back there — forest trails, a 4x4, and problems that don't involve a screen. I photograph, I research engine combinations for cars I haven't built yet, and I write down game ideas I haven't coded yet. These aren't separate interests — they're all the same impulse: find something technical, understand it completely, then push it somewhere interesting.

I'm early in this journey. Second semester MCA at MS Ramaiah University, Bangalore. I haven't been recognised for much yet. I'm building toward that — one project at a time. This portfolio is the first proof.
